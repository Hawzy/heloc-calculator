      Privacy & Zero-Backend Architecture

      This calculator executes 100% client-side in your browser using deterministic ES modules kept cent-verified against the open-source Python engine. Zero financial numbers are transmitted to any server, and there are zero cookies, analytics, or external font/CDN trackers. Saved scenarios stay solely inside your browser's local storage or in query strings you explicitly share.

      Educational & Regulatory Disclaimer

      Modeled behaviors—including open-end periodic rate conversions under Truth in Lending Regulation Z (12 CFR 1026.40), contractual lifetime caps, and the Washington Consumer Loan Act 25.0% simple interest usury ceiling (WAC 208-620-235)—are provided strictly for educational simulation. Results are not financial, tax, or legal advice; consult your lender disclosures for binding terms.
